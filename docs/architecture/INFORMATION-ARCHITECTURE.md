# aievolutionaryevolutions.com Information Architecture

This is a large educational, technology, and business ecosystem. It must be organized as a family of connected portals rather than one undifferentiated page tree.

## Core experience

The primary site introduces the ecosystem, explains the available learning paths, and routes visitors into the appropriate portal based on their goals, interests, permissions, and entry point.

## Portal families

### Robotics and humanoid robotics

Education for robotics programming, humanoid systems, sensors, control systems, autonomy, simulation, safety, and human-robot interaction.

### Wearable technologies

Learning paths for wearable devices, embedded systems, sensors, interfaces, accessibility, health-related technology concepts, and responsible data handling.

### Programming and software engineering

Courses and references for programming fundamentals, systems programming, application development, testing, architecture, APIs, DevOps, and AI-assisted development.

### Blockchain development

Education for blockchain concepts, smart contracts, decentralized applications, wallets, security, testing, token systems, and responsible deployment.

### Linux and open-source systems

Guides for Linux distributions, shell usage, system administration, containers, networking, security, automation, and open-source collaboration.

### AI teaching AI

Lessons and tools for prompt engineering, model evaluation, agent design, AI safety, AI-assisted coding, multi-agent systems, memory, orchestration, and quality assurance.

### Jarvis assistants and swarm networks

The operational portal for assistant creation, swarm design, workflows, API connections, skills, permissions, memory, logs, approvals, and release controls.

### Educational entertainment

Interactive lessons, simulations, demonstrations, videos, audiobooks, ebooks, challenges, games, projects, and visual explanations.

### Business and systems development

Business education, affiliate programs, memberships, product development, automation, customer operations, analytics, and systems-of-systems planning.

## Entry and naming model

A visitor may reach the ecosystem through a topic, campaign, partner, quick link, attachment, application, or assistant. The system may present a portal-specific name and navigation while retaining a shared identity, account system, design system, search index, and content metadata model.

Names must be mapped to canonical portal IDs so analytics, permissions, search, and links remain consistent even when the presentation name changes.

## Shared platform services

- Global search and encyclopedia indexing
- Cross-portal taxonomy and tagging
- Account and membership management
- Learning progress and achievements
- Attachments and downloadable resources
- Quick links and deep-link routing
- AI suggestions and personalized learning paths
- Accessibility and responsive layouts
- Versioned content and editorial review
- Security, moderation, analytics, and audit history

## Scalability rules

- Each portal owns its content namespace and navigation.
- Shared components must be reusable and versioned.
- Every page receives a stable canonical identifier.
- Attachments are referenced by metadata, not hard-coded into page layouts.
- New portals must pass the quality and accessibility release gate.
- Cross-portal links must be tested for permissions, redirects, and stale destinations.
