# Financing, Payment Processing, and Account Automation

Financial features are educational, administrative, and referral-oriented unless the project obtains the required authorization for regulated financial activity.

The current partner destinations supplied for the business ecosystem are:

- Lendtrack referral: `https://lendtrack.ai/join/lippytmai`
- GetBizFunds business funding site: `https://lippytmai.getbizfunds.com`

- 7 Figures Partners referral: `https://join.7figurespartners.com/?a_aid=lipshaytm`

These are user-supplied destinations. Their terms, disclosures, eligibility rules, commission rules, privacy practices, and licensing status must be verified before public publishing or automated promotion.

## Required controls

- Clear affiliate and referral disclosures
- No guarantee of approval, funding, rates, terms, or outcomes
- No automated financial advice presented as a decision
- Consent before collecting or transmitting applicant information
- Data minimization and secure handling of financial information
- Provider-specific eligibility and compliance review
- Attribution tracking that does not expose private data
- Audit records for referrals, submissions, and consent
- Human approval for financial communications and changes

## Payment processing

Payment systems must use approved providers and hosted or tokenized payment flows where possible. AI assistants must never store raw card numbers, authentication secrets, recovery phrases, or private keys. Refunds, payouts, account changes, and financial transfers require explicit approval and provider verification.

## AI swarm role

AI may explain products, organize information, validate required fields, route users to approved destinations, generate disclosures, and produce reports. It must not impersonate a lender, approve financing, promise funding, move money, or make an irreversible financial decision autonomously.
