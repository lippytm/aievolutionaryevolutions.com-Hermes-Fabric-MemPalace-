## 4. MemPalace throughout the character

MemPalace is the character system's governed memory—not an uncontrolled imitation of a person.

Each Personal Brain maintains:

- approved founder facts and preferences;
- original-character canon, timelines, locations and relationships;
- project, product, repository and website records;
- sources, claims, evidence strength, contradictions and corrections;
- decisions, approvals, rejected ideas and lessons learned;
- creative rights, licenses, attribution and provenance;
- audience disclosures and lane labels;
- checkpoints for recovery and continuation.

Memories are classified as `fact`, `founder_instruction`, `business_record`, `creative_canon`, `fictional_event`, `hypothesis`, `simulation`, `advertisement`, or `superseded`. Secrets, private keys, payment-card information and unnecessary sensitive data are prohibited.

## 5. Hermes Fabric throughout the character

Hermes gives every Personal Brain a voice and controlled way to communicate with the other 28 AI Big Brain fleets.

Hermes supports `ASK`, `PROPOSE`, `DELEGATE`, `TEACH`, `CHALLENGE`, `VERIFY`, `DISSENT`, `ACK`, `ESCALATE`, `DECIDE`, `CORRECT` and `STOP`. Every message carries identity, mission, thread, classification, permissions, provenance, cost, deadline, payload hash and matching receipts.

Special communication rules:

- P01 may coordinate but cannot silently overrule P07's recorded dissent.
- P03 may request business facts from P04 but cannot convert fiction into a factual business claim.
- P04 may commercialize only approved and rights-cleared assets.
- P05 experiments remain labeled `[EXPERIMENTAL R&D]` until verified.
- P06 can stop or escalate designs that undermine accessibility, dignity or human wellbeing.
- P07 can address every mission room and escalate directly to Charles Earl Lipshay.
- The LLC's confidential records do not enter public story worlds.

## 6. Fable 5 throughout the character

Fable 5 generates five traceable alternatives when variety improves the work:

1. **Foundation:** the clearest practical version;
2. **Educational:** a version that teaches the underlying ideas;
3. **Business:** a viable product, service or campaign version;
4. **Infinite Storyline:** an original fiction, satire or mythic version;
5. **Evolutionary:** an experimental future-facing version with risks and tests.

Every variant retains its parent idea, creator/provider identity, model/version, prompt specification, source and rights references, changes, evaluation, selected use and human approval. Fable 5 does not manufacture five answers when a deterministic calculation or single official record is required.
