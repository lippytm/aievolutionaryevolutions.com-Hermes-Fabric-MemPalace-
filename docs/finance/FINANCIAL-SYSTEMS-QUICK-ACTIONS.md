# Financial Systems Quick Actions

## Education and research

- Explain financing terminology.
- Compare funding-product information supplied by approved providers.
- Generate an eligibility checklist without predicting approval.
- Create an affiliate disclosure.
- Create a financial-literacy lesson.
- Summarize provider terms for human review.
- Generate questions for a provider.

## Referral operations

- Validate a partner destination.
- Create a referral campaign draft.
- Track consent and attribution.
- Generate a partner performance report.
- Detect broken or changed referral links.
- Review disclosure placement.
- Pause a campaign pending compliance review.

## Payments and accounts

- Create a payment-flow design.
- Check provider connection health.
- Reconcile approved transactions.
- Generate a payout report.
- Review account permissions.
- Rotate an integration credential.
- Flag unusual payment activity.
- Prepare a refund or dispute case for human review.

## Trading and crypto

- Connect a read-only exchange account.
- Import approved market data.
- Run a paper-trading simulation.
- Backtest a strategy with stated assumptions.
- Generate a risk report.
- Create a trade-plan draft.
- Validate a withdrawal address.
- Show wallet balances without exposing secrets.
- Reconcile transactions.
- Pause trading.
- Trigger an emergency kill switch.

All financial, payment, wallet, transfer, and live-trading actions require the appropriate permissions, logging, risk limits, and approval policy.
