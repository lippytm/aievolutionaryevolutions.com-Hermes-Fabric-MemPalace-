## 1. Website mission

The website becomes the public Control Tower and understandable front door for the complete Business of Businesses / Systems of Systems ecosystem. Visitors should immediately understand four actions:

1. **Learn** programming, blockchain, Linux and AI through multiple learning paths.
2. **Build** projects, products, AI agents and simulated trading tools safely.
3. **Create** ebooks, audiobooks, videobooks, games, story worlds and original media.
4. **Grow** a documented business through Intergalactic Corporate Services LLC and the clearly disclosed funding-readiness pathway.

The unifying promise is not that every project is the same. It is that every approved project follows one transparent operating law:

**Idea → Mission → Evidence → Build → Test → Document → Teach → Product → Campaign → Data → Improvement**

## 8. Integrating `lippytmai.getbizfunds.com` inside the website

Create a first-party page at `/funding-readiness` that explains the pathway in the site's own design, disclosures and navigation. Then connect the subdomain in one of three technically valid ways:

1. **Preferred:** co-branded landing page with a clear button opening `lippytmai.getbizfunds.com` in the same tab and preserving approved referral/tracking parameters.
2. **If supported by the provider:** verified form/API handoff after explicit consent and a published privacy notice.
3. **Embed only if contractually and technically permitted:** an iframe may be blocked by the provider's security headers and can create accessibility, privacy, analytics and mobile-layout problems.

Required disclosures:

- funding-readiness education is not an approval, loan offer or guarantee;
- product availability, rates, terms, eligibility and decisions belong to the provider;
- identify referral/affiliate compensation when applicable;
- collect only necessary information and never store financial application secrets in MemPalace;
- maintain consent, privacy, retention and deletion records.

## 10. Hostinger implementation architecture

### Public website layer

- responsive pages and accessible navigation;
- server-rendered metadata, sitemap and structured data;
- forms protected with validation, rate limiting and anti-spam controls;
- consent-aware analytics;
- no secrets embedded in client-side code.

### Application/API layer

- authenticated member area;
- Brain Gateway for approved AI requests;
- Hermes message API and mission-room events;
- MemPalace retrieval with authorization and provenance;
- Fable 5 job orchestration;
- CRM and email integrations through scoped service accounts;
- webhook verification, idempotency and replay protection.

### Data layer

- users/organizations/roles;
- consent and communication preferences;
- learning paths, competencies and progress;
- missions, brain fleets, messages and receipts;
- projects, products, assets and rights;
- evidence, sources, QA, risks and approvals;
- affiliate attribution and commission events;
- offers, orders and support references;
- audit, incident, correction and deletion records.

Never store raw passwords, payment-card details, provider API keys, wallet seed phrases, brokerage credentials or unnecessary sensitive data in content databases or MemPalace.
