# Creative AI Building and Innovation Process

This process applies AI creating AI to products, projects, platforms, educational systems, business objectives, and the Jarvis swarm network.

## Creative build cycle

1. Discover the user, business, educational, or systems problem.
2. Map the objective, constraints, risks, and measurable outcomes.
3. Generate multiple concepts with AI assistants and specialist swarms.
4. Compare concepts for value, feasibility, security, accessibility, cost, and maintainability.
5. Select the smallest useful experiment.
6. Build an isolated prototype with clear assumptions.
7. Teach the system what was built and why.
8. Test behavior, quality, security, usability, and failure recovery.
9. Gather feedback and compare results with the baseline.
10. Approve, revise, archive, or promote the capability.

## AI creating AI capabilities

The platform may generate applications, APIs, workflows, dashboards, prompts, agents, swarms, skills, tests, documentation, lessons, simulations, business systems, and integration plans. Generated artifacts must be versioned, attributable, reviewable, and linked to their acceptance criteria.

## Innovation safeguards

- Creativity is encouraged in isolated environments.
- Generated ideas are not treated as verified facts.
- Experiments do not receive production credentials by default.
- New agents cannot grant themselves permissions.
- Financial, security, medical, legal, production, and public-facing outputs require domain review.
- A promising prototype must pass QA and security gates before promotion.

## Reuse and composition

Approved capabilities should become reusable components, quick actions, skills, templates, APIs, lessons, and swarm roles. Reuse requires compatibility metadata, versioning, ownership, documentation, and regression coverage.

## Innovation scorecard

Evaluate each idea on user value, educational value, business value, technical feasibility, security, privacy, accessibility, reliability, cost, time to value, reusability, and reversibility.
